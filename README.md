Diwali-sales-analysis

Diwali Sales Analysis
📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer purchase behavior during the Diwali festival. The goal is to uncover insights into sales trends, customer demographics, and product performance to help businesses make data-driven decisions.

📊 Dataset Description

The dataset contains the following columns:

User_ID – Unique ID of the customer
Cust_name – Customer name
Product_ID – Unique ID of the product
Gender – Gender of the customer
Age Group – Age category (e.g., 18–25, 26–35, etc.)
Age – Customer age
Marital_Status – Married or Unmarried
State – Customer's state
Zone – Region classification
Occupation – Customer occupation
Product_Category – Category of the product
Orders – Number of orders placed
Amount – Total purchase amount
Status – Order status
🎯 Objectives
Analyze customer demographics (age, gender, marital status)
Identify top-performing states and zones
Determine most popular product categories
Understand purchasing behavior based on occupation
Find high-value customers and sales trends
🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
Python
Pandas – Data cleaning & manipulation
NumPy – Numerical operations
Matplotlib & Seaborn – Data visualization
Jupyter Notebook
📈 Key Analysis Performed
Data cleaning and preprocessing
Handling missing values
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Visualization of:
Gender vs Purchase Amount
Age Group vs Orders
State-wise sales
Occupation-based spending
Product category performance
📌 Key Insights
Married women aged 26–35 are the highest spenders
Certain states contribute significantly to total sales
Specific product categories dominate festive purchases
Occupation plays a major role in spending patterns
